About These Galleries

What's in here

Every gallery on this page is one shoot kept whole — a single baseball game, one Raider meet, one tennis match — rather than a season cut down to a handful of highlights. That currently covers the full 2026 baseball season game by game, tennis from the 2026 season, soccer and volleyball from 2026–2027, and AFJROTC coverage running from home Raider meets through Open Drill Nationals and Fort Knox.

Finding a photo

Search by name at the top of the page, or filter by sport with the category pills. Galleries are ordered newest first, so the most recent game is usually the first tile. Open any gallery to page through the full set at full size.

Private galleries

Team and client galleries are not listed here. They open from a private link sent to you directly — no account and no password needed. If a link has stopped working, ask me and I will send a fresh one.

Prints and downloads

Most galleries allow downloads straight from the photo view, at full resolution. If you need a shot that is not posted, a different crop, or something printed, get in touch and I will sort it out.
