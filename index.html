About Me

Built for
the Game

I'm Matthew Lehman — the founder of Matthew Lehman Media, a self-employed photographer and student at Logan Hocking School District with a passion for capturing athletes at their finest. Since launching in 2025, my mission has been simple: give athletes the spotlight they deserve through powerful, professional imagery.

BURST 00/15

A burst is fifteen frames. One of them is the photograph.

It is why whole games go up here, not a handful of highlights — the frame that matters is rarely the one you expected.

What I Cover

  • Baseball — the full 2026 season, published game by game
  • Soccer & volleyball — varsity and JV through the 2026–2027 season
  • Tennis — 2026 matches

Every shoot goes up whole rather than trimmed to a handful of highlights, so an athlete who played the whole game can actually find themselves in it.
